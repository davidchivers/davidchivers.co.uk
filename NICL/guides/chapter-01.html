Summary

This opening chapter introduces the importance of evidence-based leadership strategies in today’s complex environment. By taking a scientific approach, it differentiates the book from superficial self-help guides. The authors summarise the book’s structure and provide an overview of how to use it effectively. The chapter outlines how leadership is a learned skill and emphasizes the value of research in supporting leadership development. Additionally, it sets the stage for readers to begin navigating their leadership journey with a reflective exercise.

Personal Development Activities

To help energize you for this book and getting started on the adventure of your leadership journey, we invite you to start it with a purpose by entering your answers to the questions below. Perhaps you can also share your answers with a co-traveler on your leadership journey. This could be a trusted friend, colleague, or your mentor. Ask them for feedback: Where do they see you on the journey? Where do they think you may be going, and how would they advise you to get there?
