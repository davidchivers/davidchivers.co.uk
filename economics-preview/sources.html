Economics hub/About the preview

Design proposal · 19 October 2026

A proposal, ready
for a conversation.

A small, independently editable hub connecting course discovery, student resources, seminars and future social channels. This preview has not been approved as an official University information service.

What this demonstrates

  1. A welcoming landing page

    Clear routes for prospective, incoming and current students, with current official information kept on Durham’s own pages.

  2. A preparation gateway

    A simpler entrance to the existing Introduction to Economics MSc course. The course itself is unchanged.

  3. Student life and Instagram

    The profile preview links to the existing @thedurhamstudent Instagram. Its three real post thumbnails are a snapshot from The Durham Student website, checked on 19 October 2026; they do not update automatically. An Economics account has not launched.

Before a departmental launch

Agree the hub’s scope, public identity, content owners and publishing arrangements. Confirm permission to reuse the photographs and identity assets in the final deployment. A preferred Durham subdomain would need approval and configuration by Durham IT.

A starting point for our community.

This prototype explores an Economics community homepage with direct student links, seminars and a student-life feature. It is hosted separately from the existing Durham website.

Course links lead to published Durham information. The seminar programme opens the dedicated seminar homepage. The Instagram link opens the existing @thedurhamstudent account; an Economics account has not launched. The Instagram preview uses a dated snapshot of thumbnails published on The Durham Student website, rather than a live embedded feed.

The existing Introduction to Economics course is unchanged. A new hub’s publishing scope, branding and ownership would need to be agreed before launch.
