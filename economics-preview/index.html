For discussion

A starting point for our community.

This prototype explores an Economics community homepage. It is hosted separately from the existing Durham website.

Course links and seminar listings lead to published Durham information. The student-life link opens the existing University student Instagram account; no Economics account or embedded feed is implied.

The existing Introduction to Economics course is unchanged. A new hub’s publishing scope, branding and ownership would need to be agreed before launch.

No analytics, social-media embeds, sign-up forms or tracking scripts have been added. Photos and identity assets: Durham University; use remains provisional for this design preview. View sources and design notes.
