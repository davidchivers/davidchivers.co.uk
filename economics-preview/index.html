For discussion

A starting point for our community.

This prototype explores a simpler Economics landing page, a preparation guide and a mobile links page. It is hosted separately from the existing Durham website.

Course links and seminar listings lead to published Durham information. Instagram and LinkedIn are shown as proposed channels, without invented accounts or feeds.

The existing Introduction to Economics course is unchanged. A new hub’s publishing scope, branding and ownership would need to be agreed before launch.

No analytics, social-media embeds, sign-up forms or tracking scripts have been added. Photos and identity assets: Durham University. View sources and design notes.
