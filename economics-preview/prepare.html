Make a start
that works for you.

  1. Read the course introduction

    Start with the guidance on how to use the module and which material is most relevant to your background.

  2. Work through one idea at a time

    Watch the explanations, pause to make notes and use the practice questions to check your understanding.

  3. Keep a list of questions

    Note anything you want to revisit or discuss with your programme team when you arrive.

Official welcome information
Do I need to watch the optional videos?

The course introduction explains that videos labelled “Optional” are aimed at students with some previous economics knowledge and can be omitted by those without that background.

Is this a replacement for Blackboard?

No. Your programme team and Blackboard remain the sources for your teaching, assessments and required activities. Open Blackboard
