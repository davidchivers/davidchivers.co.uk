Budget sets · 1 of 4

A budget set is every basket you can afford.

Budget£24
Coffee£3each
Lunch£6fixed
The rule

coffee price × coffees + £6 × lunches ≤ budget

  • All coffee: £24 ÷ £3 = 8 coffees.
  • All lunch: £24 ÷ £6 = 4 lunches.
  • Any mix costing £24 or less is in the set. The ones costing exactly £24 form the budget line.

Coffee and lunch are treated as divisible, so half a coffee counts. The buttons move in halves only to keep things quick.

Affordable isn’t the same as best

In the starting example, every basket on the line spends exactly £24. Which one you’d pick depends on what you like, and the line can’t tell you that. Choosing needs preferences: the next lesson, utility maximisation.
