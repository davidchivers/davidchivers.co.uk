Design concept 1 · new, the lean one

Thumb Index

Every useful link as an A–Z, like the back pages of a well-made book, with thumb tabs down the edge. No hero, no photographs: a compact overview of the whole hub.

Organised by
Name, alphabetically
To try
Highlight for applying, arriving or studying; type to filter
Best for
Speed, for students who know what they want
Trade-off
Quiet: more of a tool than a place

Design concept 2 · new, the spatial one

Kit

The essentials laid out like objects on a table. Each object’s shape says what it is for: a programme card for seminars, key fobs for sign-ins, a course leaflet, an arrival luggage tag and a postcard for student life.

Organised by
What each thing is for
To try
“Lay it out for” re-arranges the table around you
Best for
Personality and memorability
Trade-off
Playful, so it needs careful upkeep to stay crisp

Design concept 3 · new, the image-led one

Arcade

Five round arches in a stone wall open onto one continuous view of Durham. Patterned columns give it an architectural character. Each bay holds one set of routes, and choosing a bay widens its window.

Organised by
Five bays, from applying to student life
To try
Choose a bay, or use the arrow keys to walk along
Best for
A strong sense of Durham as a place
Trade-off
More visual detail and a larger image download
