Three different directions for one community hub: courses, visiting speakers, student information and social links. These are working prototypes on David’s personal GitHub Pages site. Try them on your phone, too.

Wayfinder

Signage for a busy phone screen. Pick your stage and follow the line to the right official page; seminars read like a departures board.

Recommendation

If the goal is “fresher and more memorable”, Slope has the strongest visual identity: an economics diagram becomes the page’s central design idea.

Safest to launch: Wayfinder. It is the most practical on phones and the easiest for staff to keep accurate.

Most refined: Broadsheet, if someone will update the diary and front page each term.

Worth testing: ask students to find a seminar, the new-student information and a course page in each version. That will show which design makes the everyday tasks easiest.

What all three share

  • Official Durham routes for applicants, new and current students, preserved as published links.
  • The three listed seminars, with provenance (checked 19 October 2026) and a link to the full programme.
  • A prep route to the existing Introduction to Economics course. Preparation stays a secondary link to the unchanged AWH site.
  • Instagram and LinkedIn shown only as proposed channels: no invented accounts, feeds or buttons.
  • No tracking, cookies, storage, sign-in or data-collecting forms. Every page is labelled as a design concept.
