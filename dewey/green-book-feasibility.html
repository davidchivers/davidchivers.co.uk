Decision

Scope clarified 20 October: the main question concerns historical area legacy, not business survival. Do areas containing historical Green Book locations have different mixing today, even if the old premises disappeared? Keep all historical sites in the denominator. Complete clarification, competing mechanisms and scope addendum.

NARROW: an outcome-blind historical-location pilot is worth preparing, but the modern linkage has not run. Obtain the NC master inventory and its exact rights, reconcile listings to distinct sites, establish listing dates and coordinate uncertainty, and define comparable contemporary areas before examining outcomes. Dated 2017-2019 operation or building survival is not a universal requirement for this area branch; it remains necessary for claims about continuing firms or specific transitions.

Scope follows the current authorization and notes 83/88. Main retains ranks 1/2, integration and publication; its rank-4 IMLS audit is separate. Earlier notes recording an acquisition hold are historical, not a reason to disregard this authorized source check.

Match unit and outcome-blind gate

Dated 19 October firm/site branch, retained below. The 20 October area-legacy addendum replaces operation/survival histories as a universal prerequisite. The primary area design needs historical listing dates, defensible locations and independently defined contemporary comparison areas; it does not need a surviving building or a modern business at the original address. The transition accounting below applies when making specific continuity claims.

These are proposed pilot rules, not tests already passed or changes to canonical inference gates.

  1. Freeze the historical frame first. Prefer one edition, provisionally 1947, in a predeclared small NC city sample. Reconcile complete source pages to inventory records, retaining illegible, unlocated, demolished and duplicate listings with explicit flags. NYPL’s 1947 coordinate file cannot supply NC’s denominator. Do not select cities/sites because they survive or have attractive modern outcomes.
  2. Separate IDs and objects. Preserve edition-listing ID, proposed historical enterprise ID and physical-site ID. Record renumbering, relocation and parcel consolidation explicitly. A repeated name is not firm continuity. A relocated enterprise can survive while its old site changes.
  3. Use location evidence, not nearest-POI assignment. Classify verified parcel/building, block/intersection, street segment, or city/county-only locations. Record uncertainty and sources. Only independently adjudicated parcel/building cases can support same-site matches. Coarser cases remain unresolved or enter a separately defined district analysis; never silently broaden a match radius until a venue appears.
  4. Date each transition. For each 2017, 2018 and 2019 target year distinguish continuing firm, replacement business, vacancy/noncommercial use, demolition/redevelopment, relocation and unknown. A 2024 extant flag is not continuing operation; a 2024 demolition observation does not prove demolition before 2017. Listing disappearance and modern POI non-observation are not closure.
  5. Keep outcomes hidden during matching. Freeze a bounded pilot, proposed at no more than 60 historical sites and 20 reviewer-hours, sampled by historical city/category before consulting survival labels or visitor composition. Two reviewers adjudicate proposed parcel matches without exposure outcomes. Budget, tolerable unresolved share and location-error criteria require coauthor approval before execution; no numerical yield is claimed here.
  6. Retain the whole denominator. Report all sampled historical sites in a transition/uncertainty accounting. Demolished or unmatched sites receive no invented zero demographic composition. Any later composition analysis is explicitly conditional on observed surviving destinations.
  7. Do not run the modern join yet. Obtain exact modern field definitions, coordinate/identifier lineage, annual observation dates and file-specific local-processing authorization. Resolve multiple POIs per parcel and one POI spanning sites. The main project’s upstream annual-construction gate remains binding.

The Census geocoder is useful for candidate locations, not historical existence: it interpolates address ranges. Independent dated maps, directories, survey files and parcel histories are needed. No address was submitted to a geocoding service in this pass. Census documentation.

Go/narrow/stop: Go only to a rights-cleared historical frame/location audit. Narrow to site-transition description if enterprise continuity is unavailable; narrow to district description only with explicit uncertainty-aware geography. Stop a representative persistence claim if the frame contains only famous survivors, dates cannot distinguish target-year states, or a comparison-business universe cannot be established. A missing comparison frame need not prevent a clearly labelled within-listed-site feasibility accounting, but it prevents a listed-versus-comparison legacy contrast.

Existing overlap, not another review

QJE already studies historical accommodation access; JUE already constructs spatial business histories and examines urban renewal. NC and SC preservation work already counts surviving buildings. Simple geocoding, demolition counts or renewal regressions are therefore not an established new contribution. The possible extension remains firm versus site versus district continuity, with disappearance retained and independently validated modern measures. This pass does not establish novelty or a causal design. Author research list; SC official survey.
