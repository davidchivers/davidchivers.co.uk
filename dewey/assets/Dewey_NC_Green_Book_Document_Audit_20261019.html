Green Book: qualify the historical sample before linking it

19 October 2026. Public-document audit, not a historical-to-modern match or an economic result. Rank 3 remains NARROW; Eren’s contemporary city-size question remains the main empirical priority.

What changed

The NC sources are useful, but their published totals are not yet a verified sampling frame. A bounded check finds two internal inconsistencies and a substantive distinction between surviving buildings and operating businesses.

Check Verified evidence Implication
Unknown-status count The current report says 28; its tables contain 8 rows on p.124 and 21 on p.125, hence 29 printed rows. Ask for a correction; 29 rows is not a verified count of unique resources.
County scope The p.99 county list omits Bladen, but p.117 includes two extant Bladen entries. Do not silently repair the geographical scope from selected pages.
Unit/version The current report itself calls its 317 entries businesses on p.5; the project webpage reports 327. A simple resources-versus-businesses explanation does not resolve the difference.

Sources: current NCHPO report, pp.5, 99, 117, 124-125 and NC project explanation. Both a visual recount and a source-pinned table parser reproduce 8 + 21. No revised total or correction to individual historical records is inferred.

The earlier construction documentation explains why identifiers need care: different KeyIDs can refer to related or successive businesses at one place. Its physical-status observations cover 2018-2020. Its embedded 2020 FAQ reports 66 standing structures but only four operating businesses. Those are dated source claims, not independently validated counts or a 2019 sample. Oasis Spaces white paper, pp.19, 55, 62.

Why this matters for the research

There are three different questions: did a particular enterprise continue, did its building survive, and what now happens in the surrounding district? Matching an old address to a modern POI answers none of these automatically. It could match a replacement business, miss a relocation, or discard a demolished site. Selecting only successful matches would select survivors.

Timing is equally important. The current report includes a demolition dated 2023 (p.61), illustrating why a later ‘gone’ label cannot establish absence in 2019. It also does not prove the earlier business was operating then. NCHPO report.

For any pilot, retain every sampled historical listing, including unresolved locations and dates. Record enterprise, building/site and use separately. Do not assign missing modern visitor composition a zero, or treat a site’s absence from the Green Book as evidence of discrimination. Modern clientele composition would be conditional on destinations actually observed, not an unconditional legacy effect.

The next action, not another regression

The bounded official search did not identify the exact master spreadsheet or its file-specific reuse terms. That is not proof that it is unavailable. NCHPO database access, file-room access.

  2. Clarification of the 28-versus-29 unknown-status count and Bladen omission, with any corrected release rather than our own silent edits.
  3. Dated observations and evidence for location, demolition, occupation and business operation, distinguishing observation dates from event dates. Identify what can actually support a 2017, 2018 or 2019 classification.

Exclude proprietor/current-owner personal details and oral-history files. Contact or acquisition requires a separate authorized step. If only building history is supported, narrow to site transitions; do not describe it as firm persistence. No modern join is warranted yet.

Evidence rules

Separate what a source states from a proposed interpretation. Do not reconcile 327 businesses and 317 resources by subtraction alone. Listing years are not operating dates; present-day survival is not the state in 2017-2019; preservation eligibility is not firm continuity. Neither historic listing nor nonlisting identifies discriminatory behaviour or a mixed clientele.

The planned output is a compact source/unit/time evidence ledger, a scoped continue/narrow/stop decision and an unsent institutional data request. It is not a new representative empirical sample, measured linkage yield, causal estimate, or permission to replace the main contemporary exposure paper.

Calculation and verification

verify_nc_green_book_document.py hashes the same immutable byte snapshot that it parses. It requires the inspected hash and 139-page census. Using pdfplumber 0.11.9, it accepts exactly one table with the three expected headers on each of physical pages 124 and 125. Wrapped header whitespace is normalized; malformed cells or ambiguous tables cause an error. Headers are excluded from the count. No business names, addresses or identifiers are written or printed. Only page counts, source identity and limitations leave the process.

The results are 8 and 21 printed data rows. This confirms a disagreement with the stated 28, not which statement is correct or how many unique resources exist. Bladen’s omission from the geographical list is a separate visual document check; the parser does not validate county coverage or all 317 entries.

From the analysis-code directory, with Python 3.12 and pdfplumber 0.11.9:

python -m unittest test_nc_green_book_document -v
python verify_nc_green_book_document.py --pdf <public-PDF-path> --output <new-output-path>
