The adjusted venue-count association depends on the comparison object

20 October 2026. Completed empirical descriptive sensitivity, with independent code and aggregate-disclosure review. This extends an existing measure to matched adjusted samples; it is not a new index, preregistration, causal estimate or upstream replication. STATUS.md remains canonical.

Main result

The earlier positive association between venue count and racial separation does not carry over to the conditional difference between two distinct venues after additive area and subcategory controls. In 2019, on the all- eligible sample, the partial correlation with log venue count is 0.124670 for the original absolute between-venue component, but 0.002518 for the distinct-venue contrast. The original separation share has correlation 0.140757. The same qualification holds in 2017 and 2018 and in the stable-ID sample: all six area/category-adjusted distinct-venue correlations lie between -0.001651 and 0.002518.

Without area controls, distinct-venue correlations remain positive (0.397818 to 0.428344 across the six sample-years). Thus the result is not “no composition differences anywhere.” It is a much narrower finding: the earlier count gradient is sensitive to whether same-venue pairs are included and whether geographic average differences are removed.

These are descriptive point estimates, not equivalence tests or evidence that venue availability has no causal effect. No standard errors, p-values or significance claims were estimated in this bounded diagnostic.

What the two objects measure

Each cell is one recorded area, subcategory and year. Let pjp_j be a venue’s four-group visitor-origin racial-composition vector and wjw_j its normalized inherited annualized matched-visit weight. These are home-area demographic proxies, not observed visitors’ individual race or simultaneous encounters.

The original component is

B=12∑j,kwjwk‖pj−pk‖2. B=\tfrac12\sum_{j,k}w_jw_k\lVert p_j-p_k\rVert^2.

It draws two venues independently, proportional to their weights. Sometimes the two draws select the same venue, which contributes zero difference. Writing H=∑jwj2H=\sum_jw_j^2, the probability of different recorded venues is 1−H1-H. The existing distinct-venue contrast is

D=B1−H. D=\frac{B}{1-H}.

It asks: conditional on drawing different venues, how different are their composition vectors? It does not mean drawing the first venue and then renormalizing the remaining venues for the second draw. It is also not a correction for noisy composition estimates or an assumption that annualized visits are independent customers.

The accounting identity is B=(1−H)DB=(1-H)D. A cell can have a larger original between-venue component because visits are less concentrated, because different venues have more different composition, or both. The changed correlation does not identify a causal contribution from either factor.

DD lies between zero and one but can exceed the pooled fractionalization PP. It is not a share of total diversity. The earlier separation share is S=B/PS=B/P, and within-venue diversity is W=∑jwj(1−∑gpjg2)W=\sum_jw_j(1-\sum_g p_{jg}^2). Unlike BB, DD changes if a recorded venue is split into identical copies. It is a different estimand, not an automatically superior replacement for the original.

Exact specification and sample

For each year separately, report

Corr⁡{MXlog⁡(N),MXY},Y∈{S,B,W,D}. \operatorname{Corr}\{M_X\log(N),\,M_X Y\}, \qquad Y\in\{S,B,W,D\}.

MXM_X removes either subcategory fixed effects alone or additive recorded- area and subcategory fixed effects. This is not an area-by-subcategory interaction specification. Cells receive equal regression weight; visit weights operate within cells when constructing the outcomes. The years are not pooled, so there are no pooled-year fixed effects.

The input is the approved, hash-pinned continuity summary used in note 89, not a fresh pass through raw POI or flow files. Retain the same 150 matched subcategories and the original rules: at least two eligible venues, pooled racial diversity at least 0.05, and at least 20 eligible areas per subcategory. The stable sample requires the inherited valid venue ID, category and area continuity; it is not independent proof of business continuity. All 933 recorded areas are retained as labelled areas, not claimed to be 933 verified metropolitan areas.

20 October 2026. Bounded, post-result descriptive sensitivity. The earlier count gradients and distinct-venue count-bin means are already known. This is neither a preregistration nor a new primary specification.

Question and incremental value

The earlier analysis reports a distinct-venue composition contrast in broad count bands. It does not report that contrast with the same area/category adjustment as the original count-separation correlations. This exercise fills that specific comparison, without repeating the means, replacing the original measure or claiming a new index.

For normalized within-cell venue weights wjw_j, shares pjp_j, and H=∑jwj2H=\sum_j w_j^2, the existing between-venue component is B=12∑j,kwjwk∥pj−pk∥2B=\frac12\sum_{j,k}w_jw_k\|p_j-p_k\|^2. Its distinct-venue counterpart is D=B/(1−H)D=B/(1-H). This is half the expected squared composition difference between two independent weight-proportional venue draws, conditional on their being different venues. It is not sequential probability-proportional sampling without replacement. It lies in [0,1], but can exceed pooled fractionalization, so it is not a share of total segregation.

Unlike B, D changes when one venue record is split into identical copies. Venue identity remains substantive. Removing same-venue pair mass does not correct composition-estimation noise, suppressed origins, repeated devices, spatial confounding or endogenous venue availability. No iid customer sample-size interpretation of annualized visits is permitted.

Report the full family with the same graphical scale and no significance stars.
