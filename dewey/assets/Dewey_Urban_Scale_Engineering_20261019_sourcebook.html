Urban-scale analysis: file-to-file pilot prepared

19 October 2026. Software verification on artificial inputs, not a new empirical finding. This advances the implementation of the category/area/venue income-sorting question in note 93. It does not change the research ranking.

What is now implemented

The existing calculation helpers are connected to a bounded file-to-file pipeline. It joins annual origin flows to final POI metadata by venue ID, checks the income lookup, reconstructs ten home-area income-group masses and reconciles them to the saved indices before decomposing sorting. It checks both the preferred NHGIS and strict lookup variants, with separate support.

The first proposed real-data run selects a fixed, outcome-blind approximately one-in-1,000 venue-ID sample. Full-file ID uniqueness, matching and category/ recorded-area agreement are checked before any sample result is accepted. Original file order cannot substitute for a join. Duplicate IDs, incompatible metadata or unexplained index disagreements stop the decomposition rather than quietly dropping the problematic observations.

Unknown income remains excluded from the weighted calculation. Its known positive flow mass is reported separately; null weights have unknown mass. The pipeline also records missing geography/categories and explicitly marks single-group normalized information as undefined. It does not use annualized matched visits in place of the origin-flow denominator.

What was actually tested

All 99 tests pass: 57 existing calculation/flow tests, 24 integration tests and 18 independent-review tests. An additional file-to-file smoke check used 10,000 artificial venues and selected 1,036 of them using a deliberately larger artificial sampling fraction. Batch sizes 17 and 257 produced identical outcomes and support, agreeing with an independent information calculation within 3.42e-15. The runs took 6.64 and 2.09 seconds on this computer.

These timings are not a forecast for the real 4.9 GB flow file. Actual origin counts, JSON sizes, disk performance and full-file hashing remain untested. Memory is managed through batches, a disk-backed join index and explicit lookup/sample row caps; a hard per-record byte cap and peak-memory benchmark have not been implemented or measured.

Independent review found two error-handling problems: private filesystem paths could appear in errors, and an extreme scalar mismatch could make its failure receipt invalid. Both are fixed and covered by passing regression tests. No restricted input rows were opened, copied or changed in this work.

Detailed outputs remain in private storage; release requires a separate disclosure review.

This is destination-based sorting of recorded visits by home-area income, not personal income, resident exposure, interaction, preferences or an attitude effect. Fixed source and interpretation contract

  • Join ID_STORE by exact string in annual flow and final POI files, never by row order. Reject duplicate IDs in either input and duplicate cbg lookup keys. Scan the full input ID/metadata rosters even for a small pilot.

  • Weights are recorded annual origin-flow mass, not annualized matched visits and not independent people. Missing income is excluded and the remaining positive mass renormalized. Retain known unclassified mass and null/zero weight counts. Absent flows are not observed zeros.

Deterministic engineering selection and memory

The proposed empirical pilot selects IDs where the unsigned big-endian integer formed by the first eight bytes of SHA-256 of the exact UTF-8 ID is congruent to zero modulo 1,000. Freeze this before seeing outcomes. This is an engineering sample, not a national or city estimate; no selection weight is introduced. Synthetic tests may explicitly use smaller moduli or all artificial IDs.

Use Parquet batches and a disk-backed SQLite join/uniqueness index outside the repository. Keep the small lookup in memory, with a 500,000-row hard cap, and retain at most 20,000 selected venues. Stop if either cap is exceeded; do not silently downsample or truncate. Decompose one recorded area and variant at a time through the already tested information function. Do not use a successful small artificial run as a full-volume performance forecast.

Reconciliation and failure behavior

For every selected matched venue, compare each variant’s reconstructed fractionalization, entropy in nats, entropy divided by log(10), and national decile coverage to the corresponding final-file columns. Compare total known positive origin mass to total_origin_visit_weight, never to the annualized matched-visit variable. Numerical tolerances are fixed at absolute 1e-8 and relative 1e-9. A known-versus-undefined disagreement fails. Null weights still have unknown mass even if the known positive total reconciles.

Unavailable or malformed flow objects, negative/nonfinite weights, invalid labels, duplicate JSON origins, changed input fingerprints or unhandled schema values fail closed. Preserve aggregate counts of scalar and metadata conflicts in private diagnostics. Do not simply discard discrepant selected venues and estimate on the remaining ones. Withhold every decomposition when an input or reconciliation gate fails.

After reconciliation, explicitly account for selected rows excluded for missing recorded area, county, subcategory, or no classified mass. Use that priority for disjoint exclusion counts and report overlapping flags separately. A city with no classified support has no outcome. Single-group city entropy yields an undefined normalized outcome, never zero. Each variant has its own documented support; this is not a fixed-support comparison between variants.

Provenance, verification and disclosure

This checks ordinary concurrent changes, not a malicious writer preserving metadata. Bind the adapter, parser, decomposition and both protocols with hashes before and after execution. Urban scale and income sorting: prospective implementation protocol

Date: 19 October 2026. Status: calculation implementation and synthetic tests; empirical reconstruction not yet authorized or run under this protocol. This is a new branch of the existing venue-variety question, not a replacement for notes 74, 75, 89 or 91, nor a restart of a failed causal design.

1. Question and order of work

Do larger metropolitan areas exhibit more separation of recorded visits by visitors’ home-area income group, and does that association arise across service categories, across destination areas, or across individual venues within the same category and destination area?

First reconstruct and validate group-by-venue masses. Then establish a consistent metropolitan population/geography reference and describe the city-size association. Only then investigate comparable local menus and independent product differentiation. Do not estimate co-patron preferences from a residual or fit a structural model before these checks.

The previous venue-count pilot is not this analysis. Its units were recorded area-category-year cells, its predictor was venue count, and it used racial within/between diversity summaries. A correlation after area/category controls is neither a city-population gradient nor the share caused by venue variety.

3. Group definitions and retained observation universe

For the engineering pilot, use the existing nat_decile_tdw labels, 1-10, with the preferred NHGIS-harmonized lookup. This reproduces the project’s existing national CBG median-household-income ranking; it is not a ranking of individuals, wages, or per-capita income. The historical construction uses unweighted CBG quantiles, not equal population groups. Keep the ACS 5-year release ending in the mobility year: 2015-2019 ACS for 2019, as documented in construction log 20. Do not switch the income variable or ACS wave silently.

For the substantive city comparison, a local-rank definition requires an audited origin-CBG-to-metro map and common vintage. Existing origin-MSA decile labels alone cannot establish that an incoming visitor is ranked against the destination metro. Freeze one of the following before estimating:

  1. Resident-based activity: origins resident in a metro, destinations visited by those residents, with explicit treatment of out-of-metro destinations.
  2. Destination-based clientele: venues inside a metro and all retained origins, using national groups, or a separately constructed destination-relative threshold assignment applicable to all origins.

The first technical pilot is explicitly the second, using national groups and recorded area codes. It is not the resident exposure/attitudes object and not a definitive large-city result. Display this on every empirical output.

Rebuild observed group mass x[g,j] by summing valid positive origin weights whose income group is known. Unknown CBG income is excluded from numerator and denominator; never impute zero, the median or a group. Report excluded known-weight mass separately from null/invalid weights whose mass is unknown. Track strict and preferred linkage variants as a planned sensitivity, not a post-result selection. Absent origin-venue pairs are unreported, not observed zero visitation. The conditional distribution describes retained recorded flows; it does not repair upstream suppression or unequal observation.

4. Exact decomposition

Within one city-year and one fixed retained universe, let x[g,j] be valid group-by-venue mass, T = sum(g,j) x[g,j], and p[g,j] = x[g,j]/T. Every venue has exactly one category K and destination area A in that run. Let H denote entropy in natural-log units (nats).

I(G;J) = H(G) - sum(j) p[j] H(G | j)

Category-first chain rule:

I(G;J) = I(G;K) + I(G;A | K) + I(G;J | A,K).

Area-first sensitivity:

I(G;J) = I(G;A) + I(G;K | A) + I(G;J | A,K).

Both totals and the final within-area/category term must agree. Attribution between the first two terms depends on the ordering; neither is a causal effect. An optional order-averaged allocation must be labelled an accounting convention, not an identified contribution of geography or category.

Report raw nats and components divided by the same city-year H(G). H(G)=0 means normalized separation is undefined, not zero. Also report H(G), retained mass, support and coverage. Do not normalize each component by a different conditional entropy or compare different samples and call the sum exact. Income-group composition and group thresholds can affect comparisons even after entropy normalization.

Scalar entropy/fractionalization at each venue cannot recover this object: two homogeneous venues may serve the same group or entirely different groups. Their individual diversity scores are identical in both cases; the pooled composition and between-venue separation are different. Group identities and masses must therefore be reconstructed. Do not infer them from venue means.

5. Empirical stages and stop rules

A. Bounded 2019 reconstruction, only after permission

  • Hash the named inputs once, record source sizes/mtime and code/environment.
  • Audit unique venue IDs in both files and unique CBG IDs in the lookup. Join explicitly by ID, never by row position or equal row counts.
  • Compare flow and final-file recorded MSA/category metadata. Preserve and report conflicts; do not silently choose the convenient version.
  • Reconstruct national-group masses in bounded batches. Retain detailed diagnostics in a new PRS directory, with inputs read-only and no raw logging.
  • Reconcile total positive flow, national-group coverage, national entropy and fractionalization against their existing final-file counterparts on the same rows/variants. Use explicit absolute/relative numerical tolerances, record every exclusion class, and stop inference on unexplained failures.
  • Compute the accounting only on verified common support. Initial A is destination county because that is present, not a neighbourhood or centre. Produce coverage diagnostics before interpreting levels or comparisons.
  • A deterministic small engineering sample establishes code feasibility only; it is not a national estimate. Freeze any sample selection before running.

B. City-size gradient, not available from the three files alone

Audit a fixed county-to-metro definition, distinguish metropolitan from micropolitan codes, and acquire/verify a population denominator for that same year and boundary. Do not substitute venue count for population or label all 933 recorded codes metropolitan areas. Freeze the sample/weights/controls before reading gradients. Initial descriptive regression for each component:

D[m] = alpha + beta * log(population[m]) + error[m].

Use the same metro sample and design matrix for the total and all components. Then OLS component slopes add to the total slope by linearity. This is an accounting decomposition of an association, not a mediation or causal model. Report unweighted-metro results first and a predeclared population-weighted sensitivity, with support, leverage and uncertainty appropriate to metro units. Any pooled 2017-2019 specification needs year effects and dependence across repeated metros; 2019-only is the first independent implementation. Do not present beta as a causal effect of growing a city.

C. Fine-area and shared-menu mechanisms

Obtain a licensed historical ID_STORE-to-coordinates/brand bridge and audit its identity, date and match-selection. County-within-metro is too coarse to establish sorting within a shared commercial centre. Freeze multiple spatial definitions (fixed-size grids and independently defined centres) rather than choosing the one yielding the desired gradient.

For nearby-origin/shared-menu tests, require independent venue availability, origin/destination geography and credible travel costs. Jointly visited venues are selected outcomes, not a feasible menu. Restricting to a chain controls only some differentiation; it does not fix prices, hours, size or access. Without these inputs, report descriptive conditional distributions, not taste parameters. Aggregate home-origin records do not reveal direct home-venue-home trips.

D. Simulations, model and causal follow-ups

Synthetic permutations may illustrate the mechanical rise in measured separation with smaller venue samples. Do not permute annualized weights as independent people or treat their sum as a sample size. An empirical finite-observation correction needs the provider’s actual observation and suppression process, effective counts, and an appropriate dependence model.

Only after A-C pass, consider a common-parameter gravity/choice model with held-out cities. Estimate access/attractiveness/product terms before any co-patron term. Endogenous crowd composition is not a separately identified preference. Product/menu counterfactuals must reallocate visits and account for capacity; dropping venues or merging labels is measurement sensitivity, not a behavioural counterfactual.

An openings design requires credible externally driven variation, complete nearby-network reallocation and residential/pretrend checks. Ordinary entry is endogenous. A randomized menu/differentiation experiment would need a new protocol, budget and ethics approvals. None is launched by this document.

Synthetic tests establish calculation behavior, not source-data quality.

Detailed cell/venue results and private provenance stay in PRS. Release only separately disclosure-reviewed aggregates: prohibit overlapping counts/means that reveal small complements, including via differencing with earlier releases.
