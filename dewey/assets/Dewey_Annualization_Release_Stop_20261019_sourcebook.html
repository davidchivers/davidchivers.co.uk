Annualization check: arithmetic verified, new results withheld

19 October 2026. This is a completed local calculation and a failed disclosure release, not a new economic finding. The earlier published results remain unchanged. This note adds an audit outcome; it does not certify the upstream pipeline or supersede earlier scientific protocols.

The question

Do the rules used to expand incompletely observed venues to a full year help explain why visit-weighted diversity differs from the equally weighted venue average? This is a measurement check within ranks 1 and 2, not a new attitude regression or an estimate of preferences.

For each retained venue, the planned check keeps its stored diversity indices and analysis sample fixed. It compares equal venue weights, the stored annualized matched-visit weight, and that stored weight multiplied by observed weeks divided by 52. The last quantity undoes the documented expansion in the analysis weight; it is not independently verified raw visits. It cannot repair missing weeks, panel selection or suppressed origins. Recorded 53-week values are retained, not capped or reinterpreted as 52.

The same three approved final POI files were read locally without alteration. Their full fingerprints match the previous release. The run retained the changing common sample and the stable-ID sample for all three years, both racial and home-area income diversity, and all three weighting choices. That is 36 planned means, 12 weighting-gap accounts and 12 cell-decomposition accounts. None of these new outcome values is released here.

The calculation and provenance checks finished in 773.78 seconds, about 12.9 minutes. Independent Python/SQL mean calculations agree within 6.90e-13; earlier national means reproduce within 2.38e-14. These checks verify this calculation on inherited final files, not the original weekly-to-annual construction.

Annualization and the visit-versus-venue diversity gap

19 October 2026. Prospective relative to this calculation, after the earlier weighting results are known. This is not a preregistration or a new outcome search. It tests one specific observation-process explanation for ranks 1-2 in note 88, not a public-facing, attitude or preference regression.

Question and interpretation

Does the use of annualized_matched_visits rather than its inverse-transformed pre-expansion weight explain the national difference between visit-weighted and equal-venue diversity? The documented downstream rule multiplies an inherited total by 52 / weeks_observed. Undo that arithmetic, holding every venue’s stored diversity index and sample membership fixed.

Let a_j be the stored annualized weight, k_j the observed-week count, and v_j = a_j k_j / 52. Call v the inverse-expanded weight, not independently observed raw visits or a verified complete-year total. The original weekly source field, deduplication, calendar and week-count rules remain unverified. Neither weighting is automatically the right population estimand. The index still pools the venue’s available observations; changing its analysis weight does not reconstruct a same-calendar crowd or correct missing visits.

Private caches, cells and logs remain in a new PRS directory. Originals are read-only. No licensed row or detailed cell is released.

Calculations fixed before execution

For each sample/year/metric, calculate M_e, M_a and M_v: means with equal, annualized and inverse-expanded weights. Report in index points (100 times F):

M_a - M_e = (M_v - M_e) + (M_a - M_v).

The last term isolates the mechanical effect of the documented expansion on this mean. It is not the fraction of all measurement error explained, or a causal mechanism. Report levels and differences; do not divide by a near-zero gap or select only one sign. Changing the constant 52 to another common constant within a year cannot change a normalized weighted mean.

Split M_a - M_v across the same recorded area-category cells using the symmetric two-order accounting already used in note 91:

within = sum_c ((p_a,c + p_v,c)/2) (mu_a,c - mu_v,c)
across = sum_c (p_a,c - p_v,c) ((mu_a,c + mu_v,c)/2).

Here p is a cell’s weight share and mu its mean venue index. Report both ordered alternatives and their symmetric average. Within + across must equal the national difference. These are accounting terms, not causal shares; recorded MSA_CODE is not a validated metropolitan geography.

No new significance tests, standard errors, confidence intervals, trend headline or regression is planned. Earlier missing-income trend bounds and all public-facing, geography, worker-provenance and upstream-replication gates remain in force regardless of this result.

Joint-release disclosure safeguard

19 October 2026, before any new empirical annualization result. Addendum to annualization_weight_audit_20261019.md; the scientific sample and reported comparison family are unchanged. The original protocol remains intact.

Independent synthetic review showed that national breadth and all-minus-stable checks alone do not exclude reconstruction by differencing weights: if all but one venue have identical weeks, the annualized and inverse-expanded weighted sums can isolate that venue. This is a constructed counterexample, not evidence that it occurs in the actual files.

Before releasing the new comparisons, check the joint linear span of all released index sums. Within each year and each of the all, stable and complement samples, let A_c and V_c be cell totals and a_j and v_j the two venue weights. The national means and every ordered/symmetric within/across accounting term are linear in the fixed venue index, with coefficient vectors in the span of:

1, a_j, v_j, a_j V_c/A_c, v_j A_c/V_c.

Normalize columns and use a reduced QR factorization privately. Require maximum diagonal projection leverage below 0.01, with trace equal to the retained rank and the existing national support rules. This checks whether any linear combination of these releases is concentrated in a few venues. All/stable releases jointly imply complement sums, so check that complement explicitly. Apply to race and income through the common coefficient design.

Remove only analytically redundant columns: when all weeks are identical, the span is 1,a; when weeks are constant within every cell, it is 1,a,v; when all a values are identical, a duplicates the constant. Otherwise a singular/ill-conditioned reduced factor (relative singular value at most 1e-10) withholds the release for review; do not discard a potentially revealing direction because it is numerically small. No venues are removed.

This is an additional conservative operational check, not differential privacy, a provider-approved anonymity theorem, or protection against all possible auxiliary data. It does not validate scientific inference or the population representativeness of any weighting. Initial algebra and identification review

The prospective interpretation is adequate for a bounded accounting exercise. With normalized annualized-weight expectation E_a, v = a*k/52 gives

M_v = E_a[k F] / E_a[k]
M_a - M_v = -Cov_a(k, F) / E_a[k].

The constant 52 cancels in normalized means; using 53 instead cannot change the mean within a year. Constant observed weeks imply no expansion effect, including the boundary cases 1, 52 and 53. More generally, cancellation can produce a zero national difference with nonzero opposing cell components. The three-mean identity and symmetric two-order decomposition are exact. For the reused helper, visit means/masses must mean annualized and equal means/masses must mean inverse-expanded; the latter argument is not a count.

A fabricated sign reversal is possible with annualized masses (9,1), weeks (1,53), and racial fractionalization (.1,.7): equal mean .4, annualized .16, inverse-expanded 19/31. This is a test fixture, not a Dewey finding.

Week counts in [1,53] are admissibility checks, not bounds on missing visits or validation of the weekly calendar. This exercise cannot establish which weight is the population estimand, undo pooled-index calendar composition, identify selection or causal effects, or relax prior missing-income, geography, worker-provenance and inference gates. No outcome sign or size is required. Results should be presented in index points with the conversion from stored fractions made explicit; no near-zero-gap ratio is warranted.

Historical blocker: joint cell-component span

The separately computed all/stable/complement leverage gates are not sufficient for joint release of the cell components. Writing the last two columns as g and h, the ordered terms are (a-h)/A, h/A-v/V, (g-v)/V and a/A-g/V, respectively. The symmetric terms are their pairwise averages. X_joint = [X_all, I_stable * X_stable].
