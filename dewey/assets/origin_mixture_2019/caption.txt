Category-rank associations between racial and income-background mixing, 2019.

Each symbol gives the Spearman rank correlation, across the same frozen 136 category labels, of a category's racial total fractionalization F, within-origin component W, or between-origin component B with its income-background fractionalization F. Category means use equal venue weights in panel (a) and annualized matched-visit weights in panel (b). The complete family of 11 construction settings, two reporting weights and three racial components is shown (66 coefficients); coefficients are taken directly from the cleared fixed_rank.csv at its released precision of 0.01. Repeated rows are retained. The three correlations are not additive. The settings reuse observations and are not independent replications; no statistical confidence intervals or significance tests are displayed.

Setting key. The columns at left identify the venue roster, demographic lookup and origin mask. Preferred means the tdw lookup; Strict means the strict lookup. S0 is the available-2019 legacy eligible roster: valid saved preferred racial and income measures, a recorded MSA code, a mapped category and positive annualized matched-visit weight. SJ restricts S0 to venues with positive origin mass jointly valid for race and income in the preferred lookup. SB restricts S0 to venues with positive joint-valid origin mass in each lookup separately. SC restricts S0 to venues with positive mass on origins valid for both dimensions under both lookups. Own uses the separate valid-origin sets for race and income within a lookup; Joint uses their intersection within that lookup; Common uses the intersection valid for both dimensions under both lookups. Origins lacking required attributes are excluded and retained origin weights are renormalized. The roster, lookup and mask combinations are fully enumerated in the figure and in setting_key.csv.

Interpretation. Racial measures describe residential-neighbourhood composition and income background uses home-neighbourhood income categories. These are origin-based proxies, not observed personal traits, encounters or causal contributions. Shared category labels do not imply identical venues or origins. This is available-2019 support, not the earlier stable headline sample; it does not repair the failed fixed common-geography gate. None of the 66 correlations is withheld or undefined.
