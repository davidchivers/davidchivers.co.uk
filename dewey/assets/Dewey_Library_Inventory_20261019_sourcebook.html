Public libraries: a usable inventory lead, not yet an access measure

19 October 2026. New empirical audit of previously acquired public IMLS Public Libraries Survey files, FY2017-2019. This did not inspect licensed mobility records, calculate exposure or rerun the failed investment design.

Bottom line

The inventory is sufficiently stable to justify a bounded historical linkage audit, but not to proceed directly to an access regression. Of 16,603 fixed-outlet candidates present in every year, 15,864 (95.55%) pass the combined street-match, unchanged-address and 100-metre continuity screen. This is a selected subset of recorded outlets, not proof of true location, continuous operation, complete opportunity coverage or a correct mobility match.

The main new obstacle is calendar alignment. Only about 37% of candidate outlets have parent reporting periods equal to the labelled calendar year. In FY2019, 677 candidates (4.03%) have parent periods with no overlap with calendar 2019. This need not be an error: reporting conventions differ. It does mean that annual service measures cannot be treated as calendar-year measures merely by joining on the survey’s year label.

Decision: continue, narrowed. Rank 4 stays an inventory-and-linkage task, not a demonstrated economic mechanism or an investment-event design. The next useful step requires dated mobility-side place metadata and separate authorization for local linkage. It should be chosen without inspecting exposure outcomes. The earlier investment sample-size stop remains in force.

What was actually checked

The same three hash-verified archives used in the earlier source check were read locally without modification. Outlet identifiers were linked to their administrative-entity parent using a validated many-to-one join. There are no duplicate/malformed outlet keys, unmatched parents or parent-state conflicts in the inspected releases. The previous counts and 17,267 persistent keys are reproduced exactly.

The inherited candidate screen is central or branch type, not coded closed or temporarily closed, with finite nonzero coordinates in valid ranges. It is intentionally not a verified operating-status screen. All published geography, including territories, is retained; the full tables separately show 50-state/DC candidate counts. All reported percentages below have the stated inventory denominator, not visits, population or mobility coverage.

For address comparison, text is normalized for Unicode, case, punctuation and whitespace only. Street abbreviations and unit numbers are not removed or expanded. Street, city, state and ZIP5 must be complete. Coordinates are compared using great-circle distance across all three year pairs. Missing addresses do not count as identical; changes are not automatically called moves.

The 100-metre rule is a declared sensitivity screen, not an accuracy guarantee. Keeping the other thresholds, 13,671 pass at 25 metres, 15,866 at 250 metres and 15,867 at 1 kilometre. A tight 25-metre rule discards many more otherwise stable records; choosing that rule after looking at exposure would select the analysis sample for the wrong reason.

Among all 16,603 candidates, the 2017-2019 comparison has 405 normalized address differences and 504 coordinate differences above 100 metres. Of the 212 coordinate differences exceeding 1 kilometre, 120 retain the same normalized address. The largest recorded coordinate separation is about 1,402 kilometres. These are flags requiring validation, not verified relocations or evidence identifying which year’s coordinate is correct. Every adjacent and endpoint comparison is retained in the full tables.

The actual files contain GEOMATCH A, 9 and 5. The released-file layouts describe A as a street-address match by interpolation; 9 and 5 are postcode centroids, not building points. The FY2017 guide’s narrative uses a different code scheme, so the audit preserves that documentation conflict rather than silently translating codes. Candidate centroid-coded records fall from 371 in 2017 to 93 in 2019. This is recorded geocoding support, not independent validation of every coordinate. IMLS FY2017 guide, archived primary copy.

What to do next, and what not to infer

Use this inventory only as a candidate independent list. Before joining it to mobility, specify a bounded geography and service definition, retain unmatched and ambiguous institutions, check parent/branch duplication, validate a blinded sample of address matches and reconcile historical dates. Keep centroid-coded and changing-address cases visible rather than declaring them unavailable. Current final POI schema checks do not establish precise venue coordinates or an address-based historical bridge.

Then distinguish existence, operating availability, physical reachability, observed visits and crowd composition. The 95.55% retention rate measures none of the latter four. Neither matching most institution counts nor choosing a clean stable subset establishes coverage of opportunity weight or visits. Capacity, public eligibility, travel modes and opening hours remain separate constraints. This audit therefore does not estimate segregation, access preferences, interaction, attitudes or a causal effect of library provision.

Public-library inventory continuity audit

19 October 2026. Written before the new address, coordinate and reporting-date comparisons. Earlier archive counts and stable-key totals are already known. This is an outcome-blind source audit, not preregistration, a mobility linkage or a restart of the failed library-investment design.

Question and scope

Can the already acquired FY2017-2019 IMLS Public Libraries Survey public-use outlet files provide a sufficiently dated and geographically precise supply inventory for a later bounded opportunity analysis? Persistent identifiers alone do not establish unchanged premises, continued operation or access.

Use only the three existing public CSV archives and their acquisition manifest. Read outlet records and the administrative-entity (AE) keys/reporting dates locally. No Dewey, SafeGraph, Advan, ACS, employee or attitude records; no downloads, address geocoding requests or contemporary place substitution. Original archives stay read-only. Working institution-level comparisons stay outside the repository; the shared output contains national aggregates only.

Fixed comparisons

  • Reconcile archive/member hashes and prior annual row counts and persistent outlet-key totals. Require unique nonmissing outlet keys and AE keys.
  • Retain the previous candidate definition: central/branch type, not coded closed/temporarily closed, and finite nonzero coordinates in valid ranges. Report all published geography and 50-state/DC counts separately. This candidate screen is not proof that the place was open to a given visitor.
  • On all common keys and on candidates in all three years, report separate address/name/type/status and recorded-coordinate changes for 2017-2018, 2018-2019 and 2017-2019. Do not select the most stable pair.
  • Normalize text conservatively: Unicode normalization, uppercase, punctuation removal and whitespace collapse. Do not fuzzy-match, replace street words, drop unit numbers or infer equality from two missing values. A full address is comparable only when street, city, state and five-digit ZIP are present.
  • Compute great-circle distances between valid recorded coordinates. Retain thresholds 25, 100, 250 and 1,000 metres and distance quantiles. These are sensitivity screens, not error tolerances that establish true relocation. An address difference can be clerical; a coordinate change can be geocoding.
  • Verify GEOMATCH, address/structure status, outlet types and operating-field flags against the historical provider definitions before interpreting codes. Preserve every observed code and unknown/missing count; absence of a flag column is not zero imputation. Do not silently treat ZIP/city centroids as accurate building coordinates. Record the definition check separately.
  • Match outlet-to-AE by the documented parent key with many-to-one validation. Report unmatched and state-discordant parents. Do not borrow dates across a failed match. Parse documented dates, retain invalid/reversed/missing periods, and report exact calendar-year agreement and the share of calendar days overlapped by the AE reporting period. These are system reporting periods, not dates that every outlet operated or the dates of mobility visits.
  • Show a fixed candidate continuity waterfall: all-three-year candidate keys; documented precise-geocode support in every year; complete normalized address unchanged in every year; coordinate span no more than 100 metres across any of the three pairs. Keep the other distance thresholds visible. The last set is a conservative linkage-candidate subset, not a corrected national sample or a licence to exclude difficult cases from a future paper.

Verification and decision

Test normalization, missing-value handling, haversine distances, inclusive calendar/leap-year overlap, duplicate and failed parent joins, code handling, and stable-sample denominators on artificial inputs. A separate reviewer checks primary definitions and the implementation before empirical release. Reconcile source fingerprints and known totals; preserve all failures rather than changing filters after inspecting exposure outcomes (none are opened).

The decision is whether to continue to a separately authorized historical mobility-linkage audit, narrow to address-supported subsets, or stop for a specified source defect. Do not claim an independent feasible menu merely from passing record checks. Capacity, usable hours, travel feasibility and observed visitation remain distinct unverified requirements. This audit cannot identify causal access effects, integration, preferences or an investment event.

Pre-execution definition clarifications

19 October 2026. Added after the independent provider-definition review and before the new empirical address, coordinate and reporting-date results were computed. The original continuity protocol remains unchanged.

The historical released-file layouts define GEOMATCH A as a street-address match by interpolation, 9 as a ZIP+4 centroid and 5 as a ZIP5 centroid. Use literal A only for the proposed precise-code screen. Call this street-match support, not verified building coordinates. The FY2017 narrative table uses a different coding scheme; retain literal observed codes and document that conflict rather than translating it without release evidence.

The source fields define a six-character parent key and three-character outlet suffix. Reject malformed lengths, non-ASCII-alphanumeric components, missing or duplicate keys. This is an explicit conservative format check; it does not assume the parent embeds a verified state or infer identifier continuity from names. Retain leading zeros. An encountered unrecognized format should stop execution for review, not silently drop records.

Positive infinity is not valid operating service. Count infinite values separately from finite positive values, finite negative codes and nonnumeric missing values. This is defensive input handling, not a claim that the empirical archive contains infinity. Available flag frequencies stay visible; unavailable flag columns stay null.

Add pairwise counts of keys present in both releases, only the earlier release or only the later release for all three year pairs. These diagnose record continuity; they do not measure business openings or closures. They complement, not replace, the prespecified all-three-year comparisons.

The independent synthetic suite demonstrated that later status marginals alone discard earlier status differences. Before empirical execution, add earlier/later address and structure status transitions and cross-tab recorded address-status pairs against address/coordinate comparisons. These are national record-code diagnostics, not independently verified event histories.

The independent definition ledger records archived primary-document access, selected-page inspection and live-original access failures. Source copies are not claimed to be hash-verified against the inaccessible current originals. No mobility records, exposure outcomes, original archives or earlier results are modified by these clarifications.

Focused test recommendations

  1. Preserve three-digit suffixes, reject duplicate/missing/malformed keys and duplicate AE parents, and never match on names or coordinates.
  2. Keep conservative punctuation/Unicode normalization without dropping units, expanding street abbreviations or treating two missing addresses as equal.
  3. Separate 03/23 from 04/11 and STATADDR 07/15. Keep literal A/9/5, alternative FY17 encodings and unknown values visible; centroid coordinates must not enter the A-only continuity waterfall.
  4. Test identical, known-distance and antipodal haversine pairs; reject NaN, infinity, sentinels and out-of-range coordinates before comparisons.
  5. Test inclusive single-day, partial fiscal, disjoint, leap-year, multi-year, invalid and reversed date ranges. Orphan/disagreeing parents yield no dates. Assert calendar-category counts partition their stated denominators.
  6. Use a synthetic panel with entrants, exits, temporary closures, restorations, address-only and coordinate-only differences. Reconcile every transition, including keys absent in one year, rather than showing only survivors.
  7. Retain absent versus present service flags and negative/zero/positive service separately. Positive infinity is not valid operating service. Retain all prespecified distance thresholds and prohibit row/identifier exports.
