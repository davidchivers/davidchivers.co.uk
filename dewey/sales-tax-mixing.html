New idea 9; current priority 3. A bounded feasibility study, not an estimated tax effect. The measurement-led descriptive paper and finer-geography accounting remain ahead of it.

Do taxes change who shops together?

When sales taxes change, do visitors from different income neighbourhoods switch destinations differently, and does that make the local pattern of shopping more or less mixed? The sign is open. Price sensitivity may favour switching among poorer origins, while transport, purchasing power and bulk purchases may make switching easier for richer origins.

We observe home-area income and recorded destination use, not personal earnings, simultaneous encounters or conversations. The immediate outcome is mixing itself. Using taxes as an instrument for attitudes would require a separate argument because taxes can affect purchasing power and public services directly.

What could be new?

Tax-sensitive shopping and income differences in purchase-location choice already have predecessors. The potential contribution is whether that reallocation changes separation across the local destination system, rather than just traffic at taxed businesses.

The seven-lead review distinguishes full manuscripts, earlier versions, abstracts and programme listings. Miller and Omartian's mobility-tax paper is verified as a listed project, but its manuscript remains unavailable. We cannot claim that it studies traffic only, or establish novelty without resolving that overlap. This is not an exhaustive systematic review.

A complete starting cohort, not selected success cases

The official California April 2018 notice lists 15 jurisdiction-rate changes: eight city increases, five rows implementing one Mendocino county change, and two city decreases. All are retained. These are 11 provisional policy groups, not 15 independent experiments. Announcements, historical boundaries, tax bases and simultaneous policies still need checking.

An April 2017-March 2019 window supplies twelve pre- and twelve post-months on the calendar. That does not prove suitable mobility observations exist. Larkspur and Vacaville combine increases with extensions; La Mirada combines expiration with a new tax component. Net rates alone are not full histories.

Preselected local substitutes on both sidesWhether switching changes mixing across the recorded market.Needs an external counterfactual and a defensible market boundary, including entry and exit.

The untaxed side is not automatically unaffected. A treated store can become more mixed while segregation across the wider market stays unchanged.

Three outcomes, not one coefficient

  1. Traffic: does recorded activity shift between sides?
  2. Income-origin reallocation: does an origin's share of recorded visits to a destination change differently with home-area income? The denominator is its recorded eligible activity within the fixed comparison market, not every trip made by its residents. Venue-period fixed effects absorb the main tax effect; the interaction estimates a differential response.
  3. Market separation: does the distribution of income groups across local destinations become more or less segregated? Report raw and normalized measures, group shares and coverage. A fixed-group-composition diagnostic is distinct from the actual visitor distribution.

Freeze eligibility before treatment. Do not require venues to survive after a tax change, manufacture zeros for missing records, choose only events with significant traffic changes, or treat insignificant pretrends as proof. Incumbent-only analysis is separate from whole-market accounting. Millions of venue records do not create millions of independent tax shocks.

What has run, and what has not?

Completed: proposal/equation reading, the focused literature assessment, official event registry, current provider-schema check and independent design review. The review's refinements were integrated before any tax estimation.

No tax regression or original-weekly content audit has run. The existing annual income accounting cannot recover an event's timing. We need a manifest-bound audit of the actual weekly release, calendar coverage, duplicates, corruption, normalization and origin-income matching. Weekly visitor sums are not monthly unique people. A separate scoped archive-audit permission request is pending.

Continue public feasibility and the temporal-data gate. Do not interpret this page, the event list or the existing annual findings as a causal result.
