19 October 2026. Editorial excerpt from the complete urban-scale follow-up (note 93), with the subsequent hierarchy/novelty clarification. This is a prepared measurement pilot, not an empirical city-size result.

The question

When richer and poorer home areas send visitors to different places in a larger city, does the separation arise across services, across parts of the city, or between establishments within the same local service market?

The immediate analysis divides the recorded association between home-area income group and destination into three accounting components:

  1. Different service categories, such as restaurants versus grocery shops.
  2. Different destination areas within a service category.
  3. Different venues within the same category and destination area.

The components add exactly on a common sample and weights. Reversing the first two steps changes their allocation, so both orderings will be retained. The within-area/category remainder is unchanged. These are not shares of three causal mechanisms or evidence of preferences for particular co-patrons.

What is ready, and what is not

The file-to-file engineering pilot is now implemented and passes 99 artificial-data tests. It joins by venue ID, checks full-file rosters and metadata, excludes missing income and reconciles both lookup variants to the inherited indices before decomposition. A fixed outcome-blind sample limits the first proposed real run. Tests with 10,000 artificial venues agree across two batch sizes and with independent arithmetic.

No real annual-flow/ACS reconstruction or city-size regression has run under this pilot. The named processing approval remains pending. Artificial timings are not a full-data forecast, and passing software tests do not certify the inherited data. The separate missing-income bounds are a completed diagnostic on final scalar indices, not a substitute for this reconstruction.

County geography is an engineering pilot, not a test of a shared shopping centre. Jointly visited venues do not reveal the full feasible menu. Annual origin weights are not independent people or a valid multinomial sample size. More elaborate modelling remains conditional on passing the measurement gates.

The important boundary

Neither novelty nor identification is established. The closest papers require a direct method-level comparison before claiming a new explanation of urban segregation. A residual venue component does not identify preferences for the income of fellow customers; access, prices, products and recording can matter. Nature paper and January 2026 choice-model manuscript.

The inspected 2019 final schema contains no explicit parent-child hierarchy, coordinates, brand or chain fields. This does not show they are unavailable elsewhere. A centre analysis needs an independent dated inventory and a parent-child overlap audit before summing visits. Defining centres using clientele similarity would make the comparison partly outcome-defined.

Home-area income labels are not visitors’ personal incomes. Reported annual composition is not simultaneous contact. Keep the independent original- construction audit alongside this pilot, and retain all earlier failed public-facing, attitude and event-design gates. The next step is the bounded reconstruction, not another unrestricted search for significant regressions.
