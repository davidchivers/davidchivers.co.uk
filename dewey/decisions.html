Recommendation

Keep one measurement-led descriptive paper as the lead, with Green Book as a separate conditional alternative. Do not turn every available dataset into another regression. The strongest new evidence concerns the difference between the typical venue and the typical recorded visit, not a demonstrated effect of public-facing work on attitudes.

The new service comparison finds that coverage restrictions barely change most category rankings, whereas weighting changes them appreciably. The income-background weighting difference is mainly within recorded area-service cells. This is worth investigating, but inherited visit weights and the precision/suppression of origin shares must be checked before calling it a substantive integration mechanism. A measurement identity or a ranking catalogue alone is not a sufficient economics-paper contribution.

The ranking remains an order of next-investigation value, not expected statistical significance. All eight directions have now received a bounded feasibility or measurement assessment. Only the authorized descriptive work has produced new empirical results; several other checks establish why an estimate would not currently be interpretable.

What ranks 5-8 add beyond another literature list

Incoming and outgoing: a matching problem, not two national effects

When the same flows and venue score enter both definitions, residents’ flow-weighted national exposure equals the national destination counterpart by construction. Local differences can be meaningful, but final venue aggregates cannot recover them. An artificial example retains origin totals, destination totals and destination group shares while reversing a pair of origins’ exposure gap from -0.18 to +0.18. This is a nonidentification example, not a result from our data.

Existing county resident analyses are real previous work, not missing work. They do not automatically supply a new common-neighbourhood/service table. The minimum input is incoming and outgoing flow mass and flow-times-diversity sums for the same area-service-year cells, with matched outside flows, score support and geography. The close predecessor already compares neighbourhood amenity segregation and resident isolation, limiting the novelty of a simple directional map. Publisher methods.

Infrastructure: dates must precede outcomes

With an illustrative 1965 opening and only 2017-2019 outcomes, an event-study treatment indicator is absorbed by area fixed effects: the synthetic design matrix has rank four with or without it. This does not rule out every long-run design; it rules out pretending those observations supply historical pretrends. The inspected highway manuscript likewise distinguishes static correlation from a before/after design. Author discussion.

Reapplying the saved thresholds, Atlanta and Sunshine each have only one origin with dose at least 0.20, against a requirement of 100. Their dose spreads are 0 and 0.000588, against 0.15. They remain stopped before fixed effects or downstream outcomes. The holdout was not opened. A genuinely new historical proposal needs a named corridor/project, dated alignment and rights, an explicit outcome, and an identification argument of its own.

Weather: separate margins do not identify who is present when

Two groups with totals (10,10) and two time periods with totals (10,10) can have complete temporal separation or perfect mixing. Both give pooled diversity 0.5; within-period diversity ranges from 0 to 0.5 in the constructed examples. Multiplying annual or weekly origins by daily shares assumes independence rather than observing composition.

The current provider dictionary separates home-origin distributions from day/hour totals and describes different panels. It is not verified as the dictionary for our inherited release, so its rules cannot simply be applied backwards. Daily weather sources are documented; the binding gap is the joint mobility table and an independently measured adaptation constraint, such as dated access to indoor refuges. No weather files or new mobility data were acquired. Provider documentation, NOAA daily station data.

Ancestral attitudes: generation and calendar time matter

Selected official questionnaire text uses ages 10-19 for respondents and 7-17 for parents/grandparents; ancestor birth/age items are wave-dependent. A released variable dictionary and cohort-compatible historical geography are still required. This was selected text inspection, not a complete visual audit or verification of a usable respondent extract. Official questionnaire appendix.

The saved aligned-family support check is reproduced, not re-estimated: redistribution has 961 complete cases and passes its 700-case minimum; donation outcomes have 190 against 300 and fail. The joint family remains stopped; selecting only the passing outcome is not an acceptable rescue. Two historical checkpoints list 1,002 and 1,165 respondents at different stages. They have not been reconciled at respondent level, and neither is presented as a new current analysis sample. The agreed donation stop is intact.

A bounded paper decision

The next paper decision should depend on three questions, not on whether another coefficient becomes significant:

  1. Can the inherited construction be reproduced? Resolve any material discrepancy before interpreting weighting or count patterns.
  2. Does a coherent service comparison survive observation-process checks? The current candidate is visit-versus-venue exposure within areas, with race and income kept separate. If it reflects support/suppression rather than substantive clientele differences, state that and narrow the claim.
  3. What distinct economic implication follows? An independently validated opportunity benchmark could distinguish destination availability from use. Without it, do not rename descriptive sorting as preferences or access.

If these cannot be answered, retain the measurement/audit contribution and stop expanding the causal/attitude story. The original public-facing question remains important, but four prospective score-concentration screens failed; expected employees are not observed workers. The negative equal-county validation, multiple-testing attitude results, correction 70a and note 69’s non-FE interpretation all remain in the research record.
