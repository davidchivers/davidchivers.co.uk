About the authors

Tom Chivers

Tom is a science writer and author. He won the Royal Statistical Society’s Statistical Excellence in Journalism award in 2018 and 2020, and was named science writer of the year by the Association of British Science Writers in 2021.

His first book, originally published as The AI Does Not Hate You and later as The Rationalist’s Guide to the Galaxy, was one of The Times’ science books of the year in 2019.

David Chivers

David is an Associate Professor of Economics at Durham University. Before joining Durham, he lectured at the University of Oxford and completed his PhD at the University of Manchester.

His research looks at inequality, growth and development. Read David’s research papers.
