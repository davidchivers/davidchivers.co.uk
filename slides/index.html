MAKE YOUR OWN

Three steps to a first draft

  1. 01

    Install Quarto

    Download Quarto to build your slides. You can edit the source in VS Code or another text editor. Students only need a browser to view the finished presentation.

  2. 02

    Give your AI assistant the skill

    Unzip the Durham skill into your working folder. Add your existing PowerPoint, Beamer file or lecture outline. Ask the assistant to read the included SKILL.md.

  3. 03

    Ask, preview, edit

    Try the prompt below, then check the content and layout in your browser. Keep the editable .qmd source; share the rendered .html presentation.

A prompt to get started

Read durham-quarto-slides/SKILL.md and use it to turn my attached slides or outline into a Quarto presentation. Use the Durham template, preserve the teaching content, tidy obvious typos and keep the slides clear. Give me the editable source and an HTML presentation. Check every slide for crowding, missing figures and broken links.
