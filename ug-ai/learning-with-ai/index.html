Using AI to help you learn

Ask better questions, practise an idea and check the answer.

What we’ll cover

  • Ask for an explanation, a hint or a practice question rather than an immediate solution.
  • Improve a conversation by supplying context and saying what you do not understand.
  • Check claims and calculations, then explain the idea without the chatbot.

Try it

Ask for a hint, then try it yourself

Use the prompt below for a practice question. Work through it, then close the chat and explain the idea in your own words. Check your explanation against your course material.

Give me one introductory economics practice question about a shift in demand. Do not give me the answer. Wait for my attempt, then give one hint if I need it. At the end, ask me to explain the reasoning in my own words.
A simple check before trusting an answer

Open the original source for a factual claim. Recalculate a numerical result. Compare an explanation with your course notes. Asking the same chatbot whether it is sure is not an independent check.
