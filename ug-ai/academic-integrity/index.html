What we’ll cover

  • Read the AI instructions for the particular assessment before using a tool.
  • Distinguish help with learning from producing work you are being assessed on.
  • Follow the required acknowledgement and referencing guidance, and check what you are permitted to upload.

Try it

What would you need to know?

Consider three requests: explain a concept; rewrite a paragraph for an assessed essay; generate a complete answer. Before deciding whether each is permitted, identify the assessment instructions you would need to check. If the instructions are unclear, ask the module team.

Durham's assessment categories

Durham's published guidance distinguishes No GenAI Allowed, Selective, Allowed and Embedded assessments. What is permitted depends on the nature and purpose of the assessment. Follow your assessment brief and module guidance; this introductory course does not grant permission to use AI in assessed work.
