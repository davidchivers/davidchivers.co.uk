Time table

Put your teaching timetable into Outlook in one go.

  1. Copy your timetable

    Open the Durham staff timetable. Enter your CIS username (your university login code), select your term or weeks, all relevant days and hours, and choose List view. Display it and copy the whole report, including the weekday headings.

  2. Ask Copilot

    Copy the prompt below and paste it into Microsoft Copilot, signed in with your Durham account. Paste your timetable underneath. Say if you only teach part of a module.

    Open Copilot ↗

    The prompt includes week dates and common building names.

    View prompt or copy manually Download prompt as text

  3. Check and download

    Check Copilot’s event list against your timetable, especially the dates, times and rooms. Download the calendar file (.ics) it creates.

  4. Add it to Outlook

    In Outlook on the web: Calendar → Add calendar → Upload from file. Choose the .ics file and your calendar, import it, then check the events appear correctly.

Import once to avoid duplicates. This is a one-off copy: later timetable changes won’t update your calendar automatically.

Dates and room names

For 2026–27, timetable week 12 starts on Monday 5 October 2026. Week numbers continue through the holidays. The prompt uses only each activity’s listed weeks and flags unknown building codes.
